The Experience

How It Works

  • 01

    Initial Contact

    After reaching out, I'll respond within 24 hours. We'll talk about your vision and how you want the film to feel.

  • 02

    Tailored Proposal

    Based on our conversation, I'll build a collection that fits your story, timeline, and priorities perfectly.

  • 03

    Booking

    Contract and deposit are handled online — simple, seamless, and your date is officially secured.

  • 04

    Planning & Prep

    Questionnaires, follow-up calls, and timeline reviews — we'll nail every detail before the big day.

  • 05

    Your Wedding Day

    The wait is over. I'll be there capturing every moment — authentically, unobtrusively, and beautifully.

  • 06

    Final Delivery

    Your film arrives in a private online gallery within 4–6 weeks, ready to relive and share forever.

Behind the Camera

Hi, I'm Ty.


After years of filming sports and short films, I was asked to shoot my first wedding in 2018. It was a massive shift from the fast-paced world of sports — but I fell in love with it instantly.


Being trusted to capture the most important day of a couple's life is a responsibility I don't take lightly. My goal isn't just to document a single day; it's to tell your entire love story.


A great wedding film shouldn't just show the ceremony and the party — it should tell the story of how you two got to this point.
