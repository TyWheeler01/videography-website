Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Travel

Do you travel?

Yes. I'm based in the Bristol County, Massachusetts area and regularly film weddings across New England and beyond. Travel is often included depending on the package and location.

For destination weddings or events requiring flights or hotel stays, travel fees may apply. I'll always provide a clear, all-in quote upfront so there are no surprises.

Investment

How are packages structured?

I offer flexible coverage options depending on the length of coverage, style of film, and deliverables you're looking for. My packages currently start at $2,600+.

I send a full pricing brochure with detailed options when you inquire, so I can match the right coverage to your wedding day. If you're unsure what you need, I'll guide you based on your timeline, venue, and priorities.

Timeline

How long until we receive the final film?

Final short film delivery takes between 4 and 6 weeks. Priority delivery options are available for a faster turnaround if needed.

Delivery

How do we receive our film?

Your film is delivered through a private online viewing page via VidFlow, where you can stream, download, and share. If your package includes an engraved USB keepsake, it will be shipped after final delivery.

The Process

How it Works

  1. Initial Contact — After reaching out, I'll respond within 24 hours. We'll talk about your vision and how you want the film to feel.
  2. Tailored Proposal — Once I understand all the details, I'll build a collection crafted specifically for you.
  3. Contract — Sent via email once your collection is confirmed. Simple and straightforward.
  4. Secure Your Date — An initial deposit locks in your date officially.
  5. Planning & Follow-Ups — Questionnaire, timeline calls, and as many check-ins as you need leading up to the day.
  6. Your Wedding Day — Every moment of your special day captured seamlessly and authentically.
  7. Final Delivery — Your complete collection arrives in a private online gallery, plus physical keepsakes if selected.
